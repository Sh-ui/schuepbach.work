loom

loom is one family's private home on the web, at loom.schuepbach.work. Each family member has their own space there, made of pages they keep and the pages their Claude assistant makes for them: lists, plans, appointments, notes. They open it on their phone or computer with their own loom sign-in.

What loom does with Google

A family member can choose to connect their own Google account. That lets their assistant, working through loom, help with their own Gmail and Google Calendar when they ask:

  • find and read their email, sort it with labels, and write drafts for them to look over;
  • read their calendar and add or move appointments.

Mail is only sent, and events are only removed, after the person says yes. Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising, and anyone can disconnect at any time. The details are in the privacy policy.

loom is not a public service: only invited family members can sign in.
