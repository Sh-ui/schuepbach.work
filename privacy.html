loom -- privacy

loom is a private service one family runs for itself at loom.schuepbach.work. Only family members with their own loom sign-in use it.

Google data

A family member can choose to connect their own Google account to loom. Loom then uses Gmail and Google Calendar access only to do what that person asks, for that person: finding, reading, labeling and drafting their own email, and reading and updating their own calendar. Mail is sent, and events are removed, only after the person says yes.

  • Google data stays in that person's own Google account. Loom keeps only the permission Google gives it, on a computer the family runs, and short notes in the person's own loom when they ask for them.
  • Loom never sells, shares or shows anyone's Google data to anyone else, and never uses it for advertising.
  • Anyone can disconnect at any time, from their loom account page or from their Google account's security settings. Disconnecting removes loom's permission.

loom's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
